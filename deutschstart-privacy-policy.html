DeutschStart A1 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

DeutschStart A1 is a German-language learning app published by Tafil Apps Studio. This policy describes how the Android app handles information.

No account required

The app does not require an account, email address or password to use its learning content.

Learning progress

Lesson progress and learning preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not send this progress to a developer-operated server. You can clear app data through Android settings. If you export or import a progress backup, you choose where the file is stored. Depending on your device settings, Android backup and device-transfer services may also back up or transfer app data.

Premium purchases

Optional Premium access is a one-time in-app purchase processed through Google Play. Google handles payment details under its own privacy terms. The app checks purchase status through Google Play Billing to enable or restore Premium access; the developer does not receive your payment card number through the app.

Speech playback

The app uses your Android device's text-to-speech service for German pronunciation. Processing by the selected speech engine, including possible network use, depends on that engine and its provider's privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

The reviewed Android app version does not include an advertising SDK or a developer-operated analytics service.

Sharing and security

The app does not intentionally upload learning progress to a developer server. Google Play and your selected Android speech or backup providers may process information as described in their own policies. No method of storage is completely risk-free.

Your choices

You may clear locally stored app data, uninstall the app, and manage Google Play purchases through your Google account. Clearing app data may remove learning progress unless you have exported a backup.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.

Contact

Developer: Tafil Apps Studio. For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on the DeutschStart Google Play listing.
